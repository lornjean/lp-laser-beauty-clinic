A considered approach

Planned around your skin

A course of 2–3 treatments may be recommended depending on the product and your individual assessment. Some products, including Newest and Plinest Eye, may require 3–4 treatments. The number of sessions, spacing and any maintenance are agreed in line with the product guidance.

All treatments are subject to consultation and suitability. Results vary and cannot be guaranteed. Injectable treatments can cause redness, swelling, bruising or tenderness, and carry other risks which will be discussed before you decide.

Prices shown are the total for the stated area and number of sessions. A listed package is not a promise that this is the full course you will need.

Your questions

Before you book

How do I choose a skin booster?

You do not need to choose on your own. We will discuss your skin, medical history, previous treatments and expectations, then explain suitable options. The aim may be hydration, texture, elasticity or the appearance of fine lines, depending on the product.

Are skin boosters the same as dermal fillers?

The treatments on this page focus on skin quality. Dermal filler appointments focus on facial shape, definition or volume. We will explain the differences and limitations before you decide.

Explore dermal fillers, including lip enhancement

How many sessions will I need?

Two or three treatments may be appropriate for some products; other product protocols can involve three or four. Your practitioner will agree the course and spacing after assessment. You can review the complete plan and cost before proceeding.

What happens when I book?

Our booking buttons open the existing LP Laser Beauty Clinic booking page on Faces. Choose the relevant treatment or consultation there. If you are unsure which option to select, contact the clinic and we will help.

Do you still offer polynucleotide microneedling?

Yes. Our existing microneedling options remain available. This page explains the injectable skin booster menu.
