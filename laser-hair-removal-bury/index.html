What it is

Laser hair removal uses light-based technology to target the hair follicle and reduce regrowth over a course of treatments. At LP Laser Beauty Clinic, each plan is tailored around your skin, hair type and chosen areas.

The aim is smoother skin, less day-to-day maintenance and reduced irritation from shaving or waxing.

Benefits

  • Long-term reduction in unwanted hair growth
  • Smoother skin with less shaving maintenance
  • Can help reduce the look of shaving irritation and ingrown hairs
  • Treatment plans available for small, medium and large areas

Areas treated

Small areas: Upper lip, chin, underarms, bikini line, navel, nipples, hands and fingers, feet and toes, intimate back.

Medium areas: Stomach, chest, lower back, shoulders, buttocks, Brazilian, Hollywood, half arm, half leg, half cheeks, neck and jawline.

Large areas: Full leg, full arm, full back and full torso.

What to expect

A consultation and patch test are required before your first Laser or IPL treatment. This allows your skin type, medical history and suitability to be assessed before treatment begins.

Courses are usually recommended for best results. Current website prices start from £40 for small areas, £60 for medium areas and £80 for large areas.

Laser hair removal FAQs

Do I need a patch test?

Yes. A patch test is required before Laser and IPL treatments and must be completed at least 24-48 hours before treatment.

How should I prepare?

Shave the treatment area 24 hours before treatment, avoid waxing, plucking or threading for at least 4 weeks, and avoid sun exposure and fake tan.

How do I book?
