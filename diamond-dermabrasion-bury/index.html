What it is

Diamond dermabrasion is an exfoliating facial treatment using a fine diamond tip and vacuum suction to polish the skin and lift away surface build-up.

At LP Laser Beauty Clinic, this treatment is part of an advanced skin rejuvenation facial.

Treatment includes

  • Thermotherapy hot treatment
  • Ultrasonic deep skin cleanse
  • Diamond dermabrasion with fine diamond tip and vacuum suction
  • Cryotherapy cold treatment

Benefits

  • Deep pore cleansing
  • Brighter, smoother-looking skin
  • Refined skin texture
  • A refreshed, polished complexion

What to expect

The treatment is designed to feel thorough yet relaxing, with warm and cooling elements to support comfort throughout. Current website price: £75.

Suitability and aftercare will be discussed before treatment.

Diamond dermabrasion FAQs

Is diamond dermabrasion suitable for dull skin?

It can be a good option for dull or congested-looking skin. Your skin will be checked before treatment.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Your treatment plan will depend on your skin and goals. LP Laser Beauty Clinic can advise what is suitable during consultation.
