What it is

Dermaplaning is a safe, non-invasive exfoliating treatment that uses a sterile surgical blade to gently remove dead skin cells and fine vellus hair, often called peach fuzz, from the surface of the skin.

It can leave the complexion looking smoother, brighter and more polished.

Benefits

  • Fresh, radiant-looking skin
  • Smoother skin texture
  • Improved skincare product absorption
  • A refined base for makeup application

What to expect

Your skin will be cleansed and the treatment carried out carefully and gently. The treatment is designed to feel comfortable and relaxing while giving the skin a polished finish.

Current website price: £35.

Good to know

Dermaplaning is ideal if your skin feels dull, rough or in need of a smooth glow. Suitability will be checked before treatment, especially if your skin is sensitive or currently irritated.

Dermaplaning FAQs

Will dermaplaning make hair grow back thicker?

No. Dermaplaning removes fine vellus hair from the surface and does not change the structure of the hair follicle.

Is there downtime?

Downtime is usually minimal, although skin may look a little flushed immediately after treatment.
