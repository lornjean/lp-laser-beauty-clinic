What it is

Exosome Microneedling combines advanced microneedling with premium Exoblanc Korean skincare enriched with exosomes to support skin rejuvenation.

Microneedling creates tiny controlled channels in the skin, helping targeted products penetrate more effectively while supporting the skin's natural repair response.

Benefits

  • Helps boost collagen production
  • Supports skin density and elasticity
  • Helps improve hydration and texture
  • Softens the appearance of fine lines
  • Encourages a firmer, smoother and more radiant complexion

What to expect

Your appointment begins with consultation and skin assessment. Treatment comfort, aftercare and suitability will be discussed before treatment begins.

A course of 3-6 treatments is usually recommended for best long-term results.
