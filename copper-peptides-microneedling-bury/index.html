What it is

Copper Peptides Microneedling is designed to support skin that feels weakened, dry, stressed or in need of repair.

The treatment supports hydration, elasticity and barrier recovery while helping skin look healthier, stronger, calmer and more balanced.

Best for

  • Environmentally stressed skin
  • Dry or dehydrated-looking skin
  • Reduced skin resilience
  • Barrier support and recovery
  • Clients wanting to improve overall skin quality

What to expect

A consultation will be carried out before treatment to check suitability and understand your skin goals. The treatment is delivered through microneedling to support ingredient penetration.

A course of 3-6 treatments is usually recommended depending on your skin and concerns.
