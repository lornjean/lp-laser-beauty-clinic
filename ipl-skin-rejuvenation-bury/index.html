What it is

IPL stands for Intense Pulsed Light. It is a non-invasive treatment that uses controlled light energy to target visible skin concerns while supporting collagen production.

Your treatment is planned around your skin concern, skin response and suitability following consultation.

Concerns it may help with

  • Sun damage and age spots
  • Pigmentation and uneven skin tone
  • Rosacea and redness
  • Thread veins and visible vascular concerns
  • Acne, breakouts and enlarged pores

Benefits

  • Brighter, fresher-looking skin
  • Improved appearance of uneven tone
  • Support for smoother-looking texture
  • Little to no downtime for many clients

What to expect

Most clients describe IPL as a mild snapping sensation, similar to an elastic band against the skin. A patch test is required before treatment and aftercare advice will be given.

Most clients require a course of 3-6 treatments spaced several weeks apart for optimal results, depending on the concern being treated.

IPL FAQs

Is IPL painful?

Most clients find IPL manageable and describe it as a mild snapping feeling on the skin.

Do I need a consultation?

Yes. A consultation and patch test are required to assess suitability and choose safe settings.
