What it is

Microneedling uses tiny sterile needles to create controlled micro-channels in the skin. This stimulates the body's natural repair response and supports collagen and elastin production.

LP Laser Beauty Clinic offers hydrating, polynucleotide, exosome and nano eye rejuvenation options using premium Exoblanc Korean skincare.

Concerns it may help with

  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Acne scarring
  • Enlarged pores
  • Uneven skin texture
  • Skin laxity
  • Pigmentation concerns

What to expect

A consultation will be carried out before treatment to check suitability and understand your skin goals. Numbing cream can be applied if required to maximise comfort.

A course of 3-6 treatments is usually recommended for the best long-term results, depending on your skin and concerns.

Microneedling FAQs

Does microneedling hurt?

Most clients find the treatment manageable. A topical numbing cream can be applied if required.

Is it suitable for acne scarring?

Microneedling may help improve the appearance of acne scarring and uneven texture. Suitability will be assessed during consultation.

How many sessions will I need?

Many clients notice improvements after one treatment, but a course of 3-6 treatments is usually recommended.
